## 4. Source-content decisions

- **Unit 5, Lecture 4 (passive thermal control):** the source notes here are about
  **spacecraft** thermal protection (silica tiles, reinforced carbon-carbon, Space
  Shuttle Orbiter, 3000°F) — a copy-paste error in the source. The notes instead
  teach passive controls **for buildings** (orientation, shading, insulation, thermal
  mass, natural ventilation, glazing incl. electrochromic glass) from the syllabus +
  the climate-responsive design section of the same lecture notes, with an Exam Tip
  noting the spacecraft text. No spacecraft content is presented as building physics.
- **Water quality values:** every IS 10500:2012 value in the notes and solutions was
  checked against the source table (Unit 3 slides pp. 23–26): pH 6.5–8.5 (no
  relaxation), colour 5/15, turbidity 1/5, TDS 500/2000, hardness 200/600, calcium
  75/200, magnesium 30/100, chloride 250/1000, sulphate 200/400, fluoride 1.0/1.5,

  detergents, barium, boron, chloramines, copper, free residual chlorine, manganese,
  mineral oil, phenolic compounds, selenium, silver, sulphide, total alkalinity, zinc.

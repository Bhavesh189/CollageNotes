## Questions flagged as unclear / misprinted (also flagged at the end of each solution PDF)
- EP-2022 Part C Q.5(b): printed Hall coefficient 1.25×10⁻¹³ m³/C gives n = 5.0×10³¹ m⁻³ and
  μ = 1.4×10⁻¹¹ m²/V·s — not physically sensible for silicon; almost certainly a misprint for
  10⁻³ (which gives n = 5.0×10²¹ m⁻³, μ = 0.14 m²/V·s). Solved as printed, corrected values noted.
- EP-2023 Part B Q.1: printed Newton-ring diameters (4.2 mm, 7.0 mm, R = 2 m) give
  λ = 3.92×10⁻⁶ m = 39200 Å, outside the visible range — printed data likely misprinted.
  Solved as printed, method correct for any data.
- EP-2025 Part A Q.4: question wording is garbled in the scan; solved as a fibre with core
  index 1.40 and cladding index 1.37 (NA = 0.288).
- EP-2025 Part A Q.9: printed B₀ = 100 Tesla gives S_avg = 1.19×10¹⁸ W/m² — solved as printed;
  the value is implausibly large for a real EM wave.
- EP-2025 Part B Q.4: printed Hall potential "4.0 MV" read as 4.0 microvolt (a Hall voltage of
  4 million volts is impossible); gives B = 10.9 T.

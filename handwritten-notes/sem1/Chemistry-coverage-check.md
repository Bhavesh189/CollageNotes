## 3. OCR limitation (scanned sources)

RapidOCR could not be run to completion: the shared VM had ~157 MB free RAM
and ~20 OCR processes from sibling subagents running at the same time, so the
OCR jobs were OOM-killed; system tesseract is broken (missing liblept.so.5).
The scanned sources (U1, U2, co, lc, and the scanned pages inside ce1/chemfuel)
were therefore identified **visually** by rendering pages to images and reading
them, and their topics were ticked against the typed sources:

- U1[Engineering Materials].pdf = handwritten notes over all 5 units
  (index: glass/cement/lubricants, corrosion, water, organic fuel, green chemistry)
  — every topic duplicates the typed UNIT 1–5 files.
- U2[CORROSION].pdf = handwritten corrosion notes (3 theories incl. "acid theory")
  — duplicates UNIT 3 file; "acid theory of corrosion" added briefly to Unit 3.
- co.pdf = handwritten corrosion notes (prevention tree: design, pure metal,
  alloys, environment, cathodic protection, inhibitors, coatings) — duplicates
  UNIT 3 file + expansion content.
- lc.pdf = lubricants — duplicates UNIT 4 file + expansion content.
- chemfuel.pdf / ce1.pdf scanned pages — duplicates of typed UNIT 1–2 files.

No syllabus topic exists only in the scanned files. No OCR text files were
produced; this is a method limitation only, not a content gap.

## 4. Garbled-source repair (applied before the final build)

`ce1.pdf` (Unit 1 lecture 1) and `chemfuel.pdf` (Unit 2 lecture 1) contain
scanned pages whose text layers are badly OCR'd. The same topics are covered
cleanly by the typed UNIT 1–2 files + the expansions, so the garbled duplicate
sections were dropped, not rewritten:

- `fix_garble.py --fix`: dropped 111 sections (U1 lec1: 90, U2 lec1: 21),
  repaired 60 paras/bullets (typo fixes, control-char/accents cleanup,
  truncated garbled paragraph tails, repaired formula tails).
- After the drops, 2 expansions that matched only via dropped headings
  ("common impurities in water", "gross and net calorific value") were added
  to UNIT_EXTRA so they are still included in full.
- The notes went from 201 pages (first build) to 193 pages — the 8 removed
  pages were gibberish, not content. Coverage is unchanged (574 headings,
  all 69 expansions present).

**Sanitizer bug found and fixed during the quality pass:** `sanitize.py`
protected chemical formulae with control-character placeholders (\x09–\x0d,
\x1c–\x1f), but Python's `\s` / `strip()` treats those as whitespace, so the
placeholders were silently eaten and **8 formulae were deleted from every
rendered page: MgCl2, FeCl3, Ca(OH)2, Mg(OH)2, Fe2O3, Na2O, K2O, MgO**
(e.g. cement composition read "iron oxide, , 0.5 to 6.0 percent" and
"alkalis, + , 0.5 to 3.0 percent"; scale-formation equations lost their
left-hand sides). Placeholders were changed to Private Use Area chars
(U+E000+). Verified after rebuild: MgCl2 ×40, Ca(OH)2 ×36, Mg(OH)2 ×18,
Fe2O3 ×43, Na2O ×25, K2O ×13, MgO ×8 in the notes; all present in the
solutions; zero placeholder leaks. (FeCl3 occurs 0 times because the term
does not appear in any source or expansion — correct, not a bug.)

**Other factual/typo fixes applied:** source said "William Aspdin" —
corrected to **Joseph Aspdin** (patented Portland cement, 1824) in all 5
occurrences; "scalesresults" → "scales results"; "resence of silica" →
"Presence of silica"; "Sunthetic zeolites" → "Synthetic zeolites".

**Garble scan of the final PDFs:** 0 overlong tokens; all previously seen
garble words absent ("Rau sat", "eblained", "watcr", "complcte", "HARDN ES",
"x9x", "Fe+3and", etc.); remaining digit-joined tokens are legitimate
chemistry (Na2SO4, Al2(SO4)3, Pb3O4, Ca2+, 2e-, 50ml, t2/t1, 21st).
"150oC" in extracted text is a pdftotext artefact — the degree glyph renders
correctly on the page (checked: "400°C" on notes p65).

- Flags (also printed at the end of the relevant solution PDF):
  - EME-2026 Part B Q.5 says air "enters the compressor" in an Otto cycle;
    an Otto cycle has no compressor, so it is read as air at the start of
    compression and solved for the air standard efficiency.
  - EME-Jul-2022 Part C Q.3 prints "Runiv = 8.314 kJ/kg K"; 8.314 kJ/kmol.K
    is the universal gas constant, so the specific gas constant for air,
    R = 0.287 kJ/kg.K, is used.
  - EME-May-2023 Part C Q.2 gives no power or work input, so the capacity is
    stated per kW of work input (2.60 kW of cooling, 0.74 TR, per kW).

## Unit grouping (no syllabus unit structure exists for this subject)

Units follow the papers' own topic groupings, organised under the five levels of harmony
of the standard UHV (Universal Human Values) course, which is exactly the course these
papers examine:
- **Unit 1 — Value Education, Self-Exploration and Human Aspirations** (8 topics):
  need for value education, self-exploration, natural acceptance, human aspirations,
  wealth vs prosperity, right understanding, comprehensive human goal, knowing ourself.
- **Unit 2 — Harmony in the Human Being (Self and Body)** (7 topics): Self and Body,
  needs of Self vs Body, activities in 'I', preconditioning and sensation, harmony in the
  Self, Sanyam, animal vs human consciousness.
- **Unit 3 — Harmony in the Family and in Society** (7 topics): family as basic unit,
  values in relationships (foundation value trust, complete value love, nine feelings),
  trust and respect (incl. respect on the basis of 'I'), care/guidance/glory/gratitude,
  justice, right understanding → family → society, programs for the comprehensive human goal.
- **Unit 4 — Harmony in Nature and Existence** (4 topics): harmony in nature, four orders,
  existence as co-existence / units in space, holistic technology and its evaluation criteria.
- **Unit 5 — Professional Ethics and Harmony in Practice** (4 topics): professional ethics
  and ethical competence, right understanding / right relationship / physical facilities
  (correct order), Delhi air pollution case study, harmony at all five levels (revision).

## Source corrections applied (verified against the source text)

- **Multiplication factors for CaCO3 equivalents** — the correct factor is
  100 / molecular weight (= 50 / equivalent weight): Ca(HCO3)2 0.617,
  Mg(HCO3)2 0.685, **CaCl2 0.90 (not 0.45), MgCl2 1.05 (not 0.526)**,
  CaSO4 0.735, MgSO4 0.833, CaCO3 1.0, MgCO3 1.19. Verified against
  `CHEMISTRY UNIT 1.txt` (MgCl2 100/95, CaCl2 100/111). This correction
  changes Jun-2022 Q3(A) to total hardness 17.28 mg/L and May-2019 Q6 to
  100 ppm.
- Boiler term is **"bagging"**, not "begging" (source misprint).
- Pitting corrosion overall reaction: 2Fe + O2 + 2H2O → 2Fe(OH)2.
- Calgon conditioning reaction repaired from the source's own clean Q&A
  version of the same paragraph: 2CaSO4 + [Na4(PO3)6]2- gives
  [Ca2(PO3)6]2- + 2Na2SO4 (the typed source's formula was mangled by its
  own OCR).
- Carbonate / phosphate / sodium aluminate conditioning reactions repaired
  (ce1.pdf mangled subscripts): CaSO4 + Na2CO3 gives CaCO3 + Na2SO4;
  3MCl2 + 2Na3PO4 gives M3(PO4)2 + 6NaCl; NaAlO2 + 2H2O gives NaOH + Al(OH)3.

  the Beer-Lambert law and flagged in the solution PDF.

  given as structures only, no text. Structures described in words in the

  isolated C=C → rules do not apply, λmax ≈ 190 nm.

  solution (hardness values taken as CaCO3 equivalents; SiO2/NaCl ignored).

## Source quirks found while reading

- The source repeats the listening-barriers answer twice (Section B Q1 and
  Section C Q6) and repeats the formal/informal letter difference (A9, B4).
  Each is covered once, in full, in the notes.
- The source's reading-importance text is printed twice (pp. 49-50 of the
  source PDF, repeated verbatim). Covered once.
- The source's Section A question list and its answer list are in slightly
  different order (Q4/Q5 swap: articulation vs benefits of listening).
  Both are covered.
- **One source error corrected:** Section B Q15(d) — "If I liked it, I
  ......... it. (buy)" — the source's printed answer is "would have
  bought". That is wrong: the second conditional takes "would buy"
  ("would have bought" needs "If I had liked it"). The notes give the
  correct form and flag the source's error in the Conditional Sentences
  topic.

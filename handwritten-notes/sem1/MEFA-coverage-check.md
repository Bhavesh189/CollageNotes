## Source errors found and corrected (noted in the notes themselves)

1. **BEP pen problem (IMP Q and A Ans. 9):** the source prints BEP 10,000 units by
   using Rs. 12 in the denominator. With the given price of Rs. 10, contribution
   = 10 − 2 = Rs. 8, BEP = 1,00,000 / 8 = **12,500 pens**, BEP sales Rs. 1,25,000.
   The correction and the reason are printed in the Unit 3 BEP topic and in the
   2026 Part C Q3 solution.
2. **Star Traders (m53):** the source prints Sales Rs. 2,30,000, but then the trial
   balance credit side would total Rs. 4,21,500 against debit Rs. 2,91,500. The
   stated trial balance total Rs. 2,91,500 is consistent only with Sales
   **Rs. 1,00,000**, which is used in the solution. The discrepancy is noted in
   the Unit 5 final-accounts topic.
3. **Abdul Hanan and Co. (m53 Problem 1):** the trial balance is not printed in
   the source, so the full trading account cannot be reconstructed; only the
   adjustments and workings are reproduced, and this is noted in the topic.
